KEEPASS-COMPATIBLE · iOS 26 OR LATER

Your database is decrypted only on your device

KeyDBX is a KeePass-compatible password manager for iOS. The developer runs no servers and never sees your data.

See featuresSupport and bug reports

  • Reads and writes KDBX 4.0 and 4.1
  • No developer servers
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking in the app
  • Interface in English and Traditional Chinese

Features

Your desktop database, ready on iPhone

  • KeePass databases

    Reads and writes KDBX 4.0 and 4.1 with a master password and key file. Saving changes only what you edited, so KeePass and KeePassXC keep opening the same file.

  • AutoFill

    Fills usernames, passwords, and two-step verification codes in Safari and other apps. When you create an account or change a password, you can save the new password to KeyDBX (iOS 26.2 or later).

  • Passkeys

    Creates and uses passkeys for websites. The storage format is compatible with KeePassXC 2.7.12 or later.

  • Verification codes

    Shows TOTP codes, including Steam codes. Set them up by scanning a QR code with the camera or from a photo, or by opening an otpauth:// link.

  • Face ID

    Unlocks the app and AutoFill with Face ID, so you do not type the master password every time.

  • Dropbox sync

    Opens and saves databases on Dropbox directly, without the Dropbox app. When both sides change, Dropbox keeps a separate copy and no version is overwritten.

  • Files and iCloud Drive

    Opens databases in the Files app, including iCloud Drive and other cloud storage.

  • Works offline

    Unlocks and edits a Dropbox database without a network connection and uploads the changes when you are back online.

  • Privacy protection

    Auto-lock, hidden content in the app switcher, and hidden passwords during screen recording. Copied passwords stay on this device and are cleared after 60 seconds.

More features

  • Password generator with a strength indicator.
  • Entry history: view, compare, and restore earlier versions.
  • Import from and export to other password managers with Credential Exchange.
  • Interface languages: English and Traditional Chinese.

Requirements

iOS 26 or later. Saving new passwords requires iOS 26.2 or later.

Not supported

  • KDBX 3.1 and KeePass 1.x databases.
  • YubiKey and HOTP.
  • Direct connections to cloud services other than Dropbox. Open files in other cloud storage through the Files app.

KeyDBX does not collect your data

The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The app itself connects to the network only when you use Dropbox, and only to Dropbox.
